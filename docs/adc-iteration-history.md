# From the behavioral model to chip-level post-layout verification

This history follows the 8-bit, 10 MS/s differential ADC through the frozen chip view `sar8_adc_chip/layout_full_fill5_r6_seal`. The evidence cutoff is the merge of PR #49 on 2026-11-23. The final corner runs model the filled ADC core with extracted wiring and a reference/package network. Separate runs add the chip VIN or output path. The physical checks apply to the assembled chip. [Status and open checks](../Layout/results/current.json) · [Baseline commit](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/ee32663)

## August–September · Establishing the model and sampling-error budget

The project began with a 10-bit behavioral target. An ideal converter could test quantization but could not tell how much dynamic performance each noise allocation consumed. A clocked Verilog-A bench enabled sampling, comparator and reference noise on the same stimulus. In 16,384 fixed-seed samples, SNDR was 58.059 dB against a 58.066 dB prediction. This validated the three-source noise calculation. Jitter, offset and settling still needed their own experiments. [Behavioral baseline](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/0716c25) · [Noise experiment and result](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/blob/4e516f4/plan/2026-08-30-veriloga-allocated-noise/plan.md)

With transistor switches, held-sample error persisted at nominal conditions. Forty TT cases compared the complete Bootstrap driver and isolated main transistor under two ideal gate waveforms on the same load. This separated driver turn-off error from the main device contribution and directed the next experiments toward discharge timing, isolation and top-plate sampling. Terminal stress remained a separate constraint. October's move to the flat 8-bit array changed the quarter-LSB allowance from 0.439 to 1.758 mV. [Ablation results](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/blob/2e3ae32/docs/bootstrap_hold_error_analysis.md) · [Top-plate candidate](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/99da447) · [8-bit migration](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/blob/4ced90a/docs/eight_bit_flat_migration.md)

## October · Closing the native loop and freezing B

R1 closed the native 8-bit loop. R2 aligned the reference handoff and R3 adjusted restoration timing. In a 12-frame replay, frame 9 returned code 1 with eight comparisons. A passive observer reproduced the original waveforms and exposed an ideal reference controller that rejected a scheduled timer callback on a second time comparison. Candidate B allowed the callback to advance the state while retaining the original guard intervals and devices. Replaying the same input history restored code 65 and reduced held error from −442.85 to +0.10 mV. [R1](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/0b46886) · [R2](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/2c723d8) · [R3](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/b742ed4) · [Timer fix and measured replay](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/blob/39a2ee2/docs/r3_refgate_events.md)

The subsequent native reference-control and idle-reset experiments exposed two corner-dependent limits. In SS cold, tracking current and pump-node waveforms isolated slow acquisition and motivated a main-switch width sweep. In FF hot, branch-current probes at the same reference word traced about 154 µA to off-state reference switches. Reference-switch length and gate holding were then screened across PVT. A managed SSD runner and resumable collection also addressed interrupted waveform reads during these experiments. [Cold acquisition](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/blob/77d4426/docs/sar8_ss_bootstrap.md) · [Width sweep](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/fda50b0) · [Hot leakage measurements](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/blob/288cff7/verification/sar8_ref_load/conclusion.json) · [Reference switch sweep](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/e94e802) · [Collection recovery](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/1e53989) · [Managed runner](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/b6da655)

Frozen B became the regression reference. During the November module split, the migrated transistor hierarchy reproduced B's ten TT short-test codes and event counts. The numerical regression matched 16,400 deterministic records. B's 1024-point TT spectrum measured 48.788 dB SNDR and 7.812-bit ENOB with transient noise, mismatch and PEX disabled. These numbers belong to frozen B. ## November 15–20 · Device stress, CDAC weights and conversion timing

- **Reference drive and reset.** Per-device voltage and duration scans located comparator gate-drain stress at an MSB transition. D2 MSB drive passed the five-corner comparator screen. The FF-cold DFF still exceeded its duration limit. Tracing its internal node to a data edge during asynchronous clear led to a native OR at the data input. The repeat passed 18/18 frames and reduced the number of rule violations from eight to zero. The later 45-point short PVT and noisy FFT belong to this reset-OR revision. [D2 adoption](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/c6f112b) · [DFF diagnosis](../results/soa/dff_reset_feedback_20261115.md) · [OR retest](../results/soa/dff_reset_or_ffc_20261115.md) · [PVT](../results/pvt/resetor_45_short_20261115.md) · [Noisy FFT](../results/noise/resetor_1024_20261116/README.md)
- **Extracted CDAC.** Small-bit weight errors clustered at major transitions. Conductor-level parasitics traced excess coupling to the M4 trunks running beside TOP. Ordering small bits by weight shortened the trunks. The standalone extracted transient measured DNL/INL of 0.123/0.084 LSB with 4.22% gain error. Integrated code comparisons therefore used targets adjusted for the gain of the same extraction. [Layout checkpoint](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/94e6394) · [CDAC experiments and weights](../Layout/results/cdac.md)
- **Single-edge SAR.** At FF cold, two taps could read code 64 yet leave very different analog margin. On the same 63→64 history, moving from 8/6 to 9/6 taps raised the bit-6 differential at compare from 0.336 to 1.554 mV. The later bit-3 gate recrossed threshold. Registering tap selection and allocating delay per bit addressed both handoff and settling. The adopted 45/55 ns clock and single COMMIT edge passed FF-cold, SS-cold, TT and restart tests with library cell LPE, an extracted CDAC and ideal SAR interconnect. SS-cold read margin was 7.59 ns. [Aperture waveforms](../results/design/sar_bit6_reference_overlay.md) · [Tap registration](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/c46d9d6) · [Adoption](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/commit/74be8aa) · [Run-specific results](../results/design/sar_single_edge_adoption_20261120.md)

## November 21–22 · Freezing blocks and setting routing budgets (Stages 0–4)

The two-finger extracted sampler exceeded 0.25 LSB in seven of ten SS-cold frames. Removing well-proximity terms still left 1.52 LSB. A width sweep found that four fingers halved the settling time constant, while six failed the FF-cold standalone error limit. The four-finger sampler, comparator v8 with extracted supplies, XREF routed to the CDAC and the routed single-edge SAR each passed their targeted tests before assembly. [Sampler adoption and comparisons](../results/design/sampler_mmain4_adoption_20261121.md) · [Sampler PR #5](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/pull/5) · [Comparator PR #10](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/pull/10) · [XREF PR #7](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/pull/7) · [SAR PR #8](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/pull/8)

Stage 1 established supply/reference current and package assumptions. Stage 2 connected the frozen extracted blocks with ideal top-level wiring. All three corners passed. Loading the unbuffered comparator outputs consumed the SS-cold read reserve (7.77 ns before the route experiment). The measured sensitivity set routing limits, including 9.8 fF per TOP net and 20 ps one-way handshake delay. Stage 3 assigned one fill owner per region. Stage 4 routed the 122 × 111 µm core against those limits and completed boundary and supply connections to the 9 × 9 IO plan. ## November 23 · Filled-core corners and chip periphery (Stage 6)

The early routed run identified a slow Bootstrap gate fall. The final `sar8-s6f-*` runs used the r2 sampler in `sar8_adc_core_filled/layout_fill5`, paired-corner wiring extraction and the Stage 1 reference/package model. All 12 combinations of SS cold, TT and FF hot with verify, extreme, major carry and restart passed the ±1-code criterion. The gate screen reached 0.0991 V against 0.100 V at SS cold. At FF hot, supply switching charge reached 1.11× budget on carry. The recorded disposition accepted this with 22 mV droop against 45 mV and passing pin peaks. [Early run](../results/integration/stage6_early_20261123.md) · [Final corner, gate and rail measurements](https://github.com/chenzc24/sar-adc-t28/issues/12#issuecomment-7093047607)

| Final verify run | Sample-close residual (LSB) | Plate settling (LSB) | Margin to 90 ns read (ns) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| SS cold | 0.101 | 0.008 | 8.73 |
| TT | 0.006 | 0.013 | 24.9 |
| FF hot | 0.006 | 0.075 | 30.3 |
| Acceptance | ≤ 0.25 | ≤ 0.25 | > 1 |

The remaining VIN question was whether 56.6 Ω per route and the pad capacitance required wider metal. Adding the extracted input path gave identical codes at SS cold and TT. SS-cold residual rose from 0.1015 to 0.1085 LSB with the same 8.73 ns read margin, so the routing was retained. A separate FF-hot run added the output register, pad drivers and 15 pF load per pin. Codes matched and read margin went from 30.27 to 29.96 ns. Driver switching relative to sample close remains an open timing analysis.
