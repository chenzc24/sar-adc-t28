# Top-plate GMAIN-to-VIN first T28 device screen

Date: 2026-09-30. This is a **top-plate-only copied-netlist screen**,
not a Virtuoso schematic change. Starting from the exact hashed OA export
of `SAR_ADC_T28/tb_sar_bottom_plate_eval/schematic`, the generator applies
the already-characterized protected-bootstrap and independent OFF_MAIN /
PHIB timing transformation, then removes the unrelated `XBP` bottom-plate
instance and its source/probe branches. `XTOP`, its full 5+5 split CDAC,
two `XTOPBOOT_P/N` instances, 50-ohm input sources, T28 model includes,
and clock/reference timings remain. The original bottom-plate subcircuit
definition is uninstantiated. No OA or PDK data were edited.

Spectre 21.1 used the exported TT MOS/resistor/capacitor sections at
VDD=0.9 V, VCM=0.45 V, 27 C. Input targets have 0.45-V common mode,
and the raw differential held error is
`(TOPP_TOP−TOPN_TOP)−(VINP−VINN)` at 270 ns, without gain/offset
fitting. PHI samples near 211–250 ns; OFF_MAIN isolates the main gate
before PHIB resets BOOT at 251.3 ns; BASE reconnects CDAC bottom
references near 252 ns. The simulator stops at 285 ns with 10-ps
maximum step. No transient noise or mismatch was enabled.

## One-factor device sequence

All device templates are copied from T28 instances already present in
the exported protected-bootstrap subcircuit (`MN7` for `nch_18_mac`,
`MP4` for `pch_18_mac`): `L=150 nm`, `W=320 nm` per finger, `nf=1`.
`multi` is varied only on the new pass devices. Original `MN11` and
the pump are unchanged.
The copied subcircuit retains an inherited comment saying the GMAIN
steering switches are ideal; after substitution, that comment applies
to `STRACK` and the PGATE controls, **not** to the explicitly named
`MN14`/`MP14` pass devices.

| Hold path | Real devices added per bootstrap | Remaining ideal control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline | None; `SHOLD` ideal relay to VIN | `STRACK`, `SHOLD`, OFF_MAIN and PHIB clocks |
| NMOS | `MN14 (GMAIN OFF_MAIN VIN VIN)` | `STRACK`, 0/0.9-V OFF_MAIN source |
| PMOS | `MP14 (GMAIN PGATE VIN GMAIN)` | `STRACK`; ideal relays hold `PGATE=G` during track and `PGATE=VSS` during hold |
| Complementary | Both above in parallel | Same ideal `STRACK` and PGATE steering |

The PMOS body-to-GMAIN connection is only a **screened dynamic-well
hypothesis**. Its physical well, body-junction, and gate-driver
implementation remain unverified; the PMOS gate is not claimed to be
generated by a real 0.9-V logic circuit. No unaccounted 1.8-V external
drive source was introduced.

For a single NMOS, high VIN leaves only about 50 mV of gate overdrive
when OFF_MAIN reaches 0.9 V; `GMAIN` stays near 1.61 V at 270 ns.
The single PMOS has the complementary low-VIN threshold limit. Both
fail the 0.439-mV raw-error allocation. The complementary path removes
the sustained gate-bias defect: for `multi=4`, the representative
`GMAIN−VIN` is within microvolts at 270 ns and within 0.05 mV on the
high side before the 251.3-ns reset. Nevertheless its **worst measured
five-point raw error is 0.842425 mV**, nearly 1.92 times the allocation.
Reducing both devices to `multi=1` cuts the +0.4-V error to 0.481913 mV,
still above the guardrail; `multi=2` is 0.567460 mV. Selecting from
the +0.8-V endpoint alone would have falsely declared all three
complementary sizes acceptable. Near-zero held `GMAIN−VIN` does not imply negligible
switch-off charge/clock feedthrough.

All fourteen selected transients completed with zero Spectre errors.
Every case printed four existing PDK `SFE-1131` duplicate-model
warnings; the `multi=2`, +0.8-V case additionally printed one
`SPECTRE-16780` LTE-relaxation warning on an ideal CDAC switch
discontinuity. No case printed CMI or reported suppressed warnings.
Representative T28 terminal-voltage peaks were reconstructed and are
saved per case in JSON; this is a diagnostic, **not** foundry SOA
approval. No full PVT, dynamic input, Monte Carlo, clock load/power,
post-layout, real boosted gate steering, or next-cycle acquisition was
run.

**Decision:** top-plate sampling is the active architecture, but no
physical GMAIN hold-control candidate has yet passed the five-point
0.439-mV raw-error gate. Keep the complementary path as the next
mechanistic candidate; determine the input-dependent turn-off error
through controlled timing and asymmetric sizing before replacing its
remaining ideal control relays. Do not promote it to OA yet. Exact
copied decks, PSF, Spectre logs and case JSON are retained under
ignored `output/top_plate_gmain_first_device/`; the reproducer is
`scripts/characterization/screen_top_plate_gmain_hold_device.py`.
