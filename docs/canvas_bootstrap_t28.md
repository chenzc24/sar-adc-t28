# Canvas topology implemented with native T28 devices

Date: 2026-10-19. First nominal sizing result, not a full ADC acceptance.

## Result and actual circuit

The selected schematic is `SAR_ADC_T28/sar_bootstrap_canvas_t28/schematic`;
its testbench is `tb_bootstrap_canvas_t28/schematic`. All twelve MOS instances
are directly from `tsmcN28`: `nch_18_mac` and `pch_18_mac`. The ten bootstrap
MOS retain the user's Canvas topology and body connections; two additional
MOS implement its inverter. The two capacitors are `analogLib/cap`.
There are no custom device wrappers, ideal gate-steering relays, or rewritten
device netlists in this implementation.

| Device/group | Selected W | L | fingers / multiplier |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Mmain | 0.5 um | 150 nm | 1 / 1 |
| Msin, M2, M4, M4A, M4B, M4C, M8, Maux, MRSTG | 1 um | 150 nm | 1 / 1 |
| MPCLK, MNCLK (physical inverter) | 1 um | 150 nm | 1 / 1 |

`Cboot=Cs=1 pF`. The capacitors were retained, not optimized in this round.
The 150-nm gate length belongs to the selected **1.8-V IO transistor class in
the T28 PDK**; it is not a substitution of a different technology node.

The Canvas reference was `Bootstrap switch/dut`, revision 357, structure
revision 32. Its compact source is `config/canvas_bootstrap_topology.json`.
Its Sky130 binding was deliberately replaced by the user's chosen T28 class.
The Canvas ideal 10-ps inverter was replaced by actual T28 PMOS/NMOS, so its
exact clock delay is not assumed to survive the mapping. All sizing candidates
below use that same physical inverter.

Relative placement and the main route paths follow the original drawing.
Native PDK terminal positions require short adapted leads; there is no claim
of pixel-identical artwork. Original hidden bulk choices are explicit native
B connections. Direct schematic readback verified all 40 bootstrap MOS
terminal connections against the source. Final Check & Save has zero markers.

## Conditions and measurement

Spectre 21.1.0.509, ordinary Spectre mode, TT, VDD=0.9 V, 27 C. The fresh OA
export supplies its foundry model include and the sections `pre_simu`,
`noise_worst`, `ttmacro_mos_moscap`, `tt_res_bip_dio_disres`, `tt_mom`,
`tt_ind_jvar`, `tt_r_metal`, as recorded in `config/pdk_mapping.yaml`.
No foundry model bodies are copied into tracked files.

CLKS is an ideal 0/0.9-V, 10-MHz source with 100-ps edges and 39-ns pulse
width; CLKSB is generated by the native inverter. The input has 50-ohm source
resistance and changes from `0.9-VIN_DC` to `VIN_DC` near 205 ns. Acquisition
is near 211--250 ns; the pulse fall starts near 250.1 ns and crosses 50% near
250.15 ns. Acquisition error is read at 249.9 ns and raw held error at 270 ns.
The stop time is 285 ns with 10-ps maximum step and conservative accuracy.
No gain/offset fit is removed.

The sampled load is the user's **single-ended 1-pF capacitor to ground**.
The 0.439-mV check below is a conservative single-ended functional screen
borrowed from the ADC allocation. It does not establish full differential CDAC,
aperture, noise or ADC accuracy. Input transitions here are acquisition-step
tests; they are not a moving-sine jitter or SNDR test.

## Sizing experiment

All lengths stay at 150 nm. The three screening inputs are 0.05, 0.45 and
0.85 V. Each case is exported from the actual saved OA schematic before
simulation; only design-variable values and simulator options/analysis/save
controls are supplied around that circuit.

| Candidate | Change from original widths | Worst acquisition error magnitude (mV) | Worst held error magnitude (mV) |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Baseline | All MOS W=1 um | 0.0000493 | 0.554224 |
| Main 0.5 um | Mmain only | 0.056856 | 0.302735 |
| Main 0.32 um | Mmain only | 3.307791 | 2.946084 |
| Main 0.32 um, reset 2 um | Also M8/Maux/MRSTG W=2 um | 3.395700 | 3.028067 |

The 0.5-um main device is the selected first nominal balance. Compared over
the same three inputs, it reduces the largest absolute held error by 45.4%,
while its worst acquisition error remains 0.057 mV. At 0.32 um, the reduced
conductance leaves substantial acquisition error; a stronger reset does not
recover it. These results support a width tradeoff, not the conclusion that
smaller is always better. L was selected for this device class and was not swept.

Final five-point rerun of the installed 0.5-um schematic:

| VIN (V) | Acquisition error at 249.9 ns (mV) | Raw held error at 270 ns (mV) |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.05 | +0.056856 | -0.124610 |
| 0.25 | +0.013101 | -0.191853 |
| 0.45 | -0.000048 | -0.225775 |
| 0.65 | -0.009030 | -0.260366 |
| 0.85 | -0.023644 | -0.302735 |

For the 0.05-V acquisition step, the selected case enters and stays within
0.439 mV about 32.24 ns after 211.2 ns, leaving about 6.5 ns before the
249.9-ns acquisition observation. The observed track G-minus-VIN is about
0.89 V. A Ron estimate at 215 ns is a transient apparent ratio, not a settled
DC Ron characterization, and should not be used to claim Ron linearity.

Pairing independent single-ended held results gives a diagnostic differential
error of -0.178125 mV for 0.85/0.05-V inputs and -0.068513 mV for 0.65/0.25 V.
No coupled differential fixture or full CDAC was run here; those differences
are not separate full-ADC simulations. Likewise, these T28-to-T28 sizing
comparisons do not measure a process advantage relative to Sky130.

## Validation and evidence

Seventeen useful Spectre transients completed with `result.ok=true` and zero
simulation errors. Three earlier harness read-in failures came from duplicate
model inclusion/options; they were repaired before accepting a baseline.
The final five runs each print four inherited `SFE-1131` model scope-option
warnings, no CMI warnings, and no reported suppressed warnings. Baseline logs
also retain simulator-option override warnings that the later harness removed.

Reconstructed sampled MOS terminal differences have a largest magnitude of
1.75435 V (`Maux` B--D), rather than treating boosted-node voltage alone as
device stress. This nominal observation is not a foundry reliability approval.
The selected dimensions have not passed PVT, Monte Carlo, transient noise,
layout extraction, dynamic-input aperture, next-cycle or integrated-ADC tests.

All generated evidence is under `output/canvas_bootstrap_t28/`:

- `oa_netlists/{baseline,main_500n,main_320n,main_320n_reset_2u,selected,final}/`:
  exact unmodified OA exports;
- `*_sizes.json`, `native_readback.json`, `topology_verification.json`,
  `check_markers.json`: actual PDK dimensions and connectivity;
- `decks/`, `spectre/`, `*_result.json`, `*_waveform.json`: exact analysis
  inputs, saved runs, success contract and waveform provenance;
- `analysis_summary.json`, `warning_inventory.json`,
  `selected_terminal_peaks.json`: measurements and warning/stress diagnostics;
- `native_schematic.png`, `timing_comparison.png`: review figures.

After two unused wire fragments from the former ideal-inverter drawing were
removed, a new `final/input.scs` export matched the five-point `selected`
export exactly except its generation timestamp. The comparison hashes are in
`final_export_equivalence.json`. The final native DUT and testbench are retained.

The six custom-wrapper/drawing cells from the initial misunderstood attempt
were removed after checking their ownership and saved state. Their source and
readback remain under ignored output for reconstruction; they were never used
in the accepted simulations. Existing project bootstrap cells and Canvas were
not changed.

## Reproduction

Use the workspace bridge Python for OA/Spectre operations and the project
Python for plotting. The source generator refuses existing targets.

```powershell
E:/bridge-Agent-T28/virtuoso-bridge-lite/.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/characterization/canvas_bootstrap_t28.py read
E:/bridge-Agent-T28/virtuoso-bridge-lite/.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/characterization/canvas_bootstrap_t28.py inspect
.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/analysis/analyze_canvas_bootstrap_t28.py
```

`screen` changes the active DUT widths; `finalize` restores the selected
widths, exports them and runs all five final inputs. Keep exact per-candidate
exports when rerunning. `prove` verifies that the current saved OA export
matches the selected simulation. The source/config/report are committed
locally; pushing requires a user request.
