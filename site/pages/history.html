October · Frozen B

Close the native loop

The B revision fixed the reference-controller handoff and established the first reusable transistor-level baseline. Its deterministic TT spectrum measured 48.79 dB SNDR, 7.81-bit ENOB and 64.77 dBc SFDR.

Repair asynchronous reset stress

The reset-OR candidate moved a data edge out of the stressful clear interval. The 45-point PVT run passed 490 short frames. A separate single-seed TT noisy FFT recorded 48.55 dB SNDR and 7.77-bit ENOB in the schematic.
