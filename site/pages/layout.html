Routing and fill

Fill7 peripheral loading

The extracted route corridors report 65.40 fF on VINN and 64.64 fF on VINP, a 0.76 fF difference. VREFN and VREFP are 253.31 fF and 222.63 fF. The extraction treats fill coupling as capacitance to ground, giving an upper-bound estimate for these route loads.
